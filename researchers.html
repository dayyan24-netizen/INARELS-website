Join INARELS

Apply for Membership

INARELS welcomes established researchers, emerging scholars, and community partners whose work engages with education, language, literacy, history, or social change in African contexts and beyond. Membership involves a brief review by the INARELS Leadership Team.

What Membership Includes

  • ■ Listing in the INARELS researcher directory with your full profile
  • ■ Access to the Member Portal for project-level data and materials
  • ■ Opportunities to lead, join, or contribute to existing INARELS projects
  • ■ Collaboration tools including file upload, shared folders, and messaging
  • ■ Visibility across the network's international publications and events

A Note on Data Access

Applying for membership does not automatically grant access to all shared or raw project data. Membership approval and data-access permission are treated as related but separate layers — each lead researcher manages access to their own project's materials. Once your membership is approved, you can submit project-specific access requests through the portal.

The Process

  1. 1 Submit the application form with your bio and research description.
  2. 2 The INARELS Leadership Team reviews your application.
  3. 3 You will be contacted via email with the decision.
  4. 4 Upon approval, you receive portal access and your profile is added to the directory.
