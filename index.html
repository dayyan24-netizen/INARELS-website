What INARELS Does

A research network built for
impact, collaboration, and openness.

International Research Collaboration

Connects researchers working across different regions and contexts, enabling comparative and collaborative action research at an international scale.

Project-Based Knowledge Sharing

Organizes research around clearly defined projects with rich public-facing pages including descriptions, publications, images, and project leader contact information.

Public Scholarship & Publications

Allows visitors to explore the goals, publications, and people connected to each project — making INARELS research visible and accessible beyond institutional walls.

Controlled Data Access

Gives approved members access to project-specific materials through a login and permission system — keeping each dataset tied to its context, ownership, and project purpose.

Join the Network

Are you a researcher, emerging scholar, or community collaborator?

INARELS welcomes established researchers, graduate students, and community partners whose work touches education, language, literacy, or society. Membership involves a brief review process — submit your bio and research description to begin.

  • Access to project-specific research data and materials
  • Collaboration tools and a shared member portal
  • Visibility through the INARELS researcher directory
  • Opportunities to lead or contribute to network projects

INARELS

International Network for Action Research on Education, Language and Society

INARELS is a research network platform designed to support international collaboration, project visibility, and controlled data sharing across education, language, and society. Access to project data may be limited by membership, approval status, project ownership, ethical requirements, or researcher permission.
